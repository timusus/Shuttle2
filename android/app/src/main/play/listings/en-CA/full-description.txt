Shuttle Music is a music player for the songs on your phone and the music on your own Jellyfin, Plex or Emby server. No ads, no account with me, and one library for all of it.

Play MP3, FLAC, Opus, AAC, Ogg and more straight from your device, offline. Or sign in to your media server and stream your whole collection with its artwork and playlists.

WHY SHUTTLE

• Local and server music in one app. Your offline files and your Jellyfin, Plex or Emby library sit side by side.
• Its own library, built from your tags. Shuttle reads your files directly instead of relying on the Android Media Store, so playlists don't go missing and tags import correctly.
• Built-in Opus and FLAC decoders, so they play the same on every phone.
• A 10-band equalizer with a frequency response graph, plus ReplayGain for a steady volume between tracks and albums.
• A tag editor that edits one song or a whole album at once.

JELLYFIN, PLEX AND EMBY

Shuttle is a music client for self-hosted media servers. Add your Jellyfin or Emby server with its address and your login, or sign in to Plex with your Plex account. Browse your server's albums, artists, genres and playlists, search it, and play to your phone, your car with Android Auto, or your speakers with Chromecast.

FREE TRIAL, THEN UNLOCK

Shuttle Music is a paid app with a 14-day free trial of every feature. No card is needed to start. After the trial, unlock it with a one-time purchase or a yearly plan. If you don't unlock it, playback gradually speeds up until you do.
