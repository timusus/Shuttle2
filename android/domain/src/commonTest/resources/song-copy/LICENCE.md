# pairs.tsv licence

`pairs.tsv` is a stratified sample of the song-match corpus (`support/song-match/make_pairs.py`, seed 1069). Its
strings, numbers and labels come only from:

- the MusicBrainz database export, core tables (CC0 1.0), schema sequence 31, export of 2026-10;
- the ListenBrainz incremental listens dump 2750 of 2026-10-29 (CC0 1.0).

The duration jitter and other noise are drawn from those two sources; nothing from AcoustID (CC BY-SA) is in it. The
file is therefore CC0 1.0: <https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/>.
