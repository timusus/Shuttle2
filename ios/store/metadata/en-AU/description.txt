A music player for music on your device and on your own Jellyfin, Emby or Plex server. No ads, no tracking, no account with us.

Play the files on your iPhone or iPad, or sign in to your server and your music is right there: albums, artists, genres, playlists and songs, with artwork, search and fast scrolling through big libraries.

FEATURES

• Plays music stored on your device, free and with no server needed. Add files through the Files app or Finder, or pick a folder. FLAC, MP3, M4A, AAC, Ogg, Opus, WAV and AIFF are supported.
• Works with Jellyfin, Emby and Plex. Jellyfin and Emby sign in with a server address and your login (or Quick Connect on Jellyfin). Plex signs in with your plex.tv account.
• Gapless playback, including FLAC and other formats that the system player does not handle.
• Equalizer and ReplayGain, for the sound you want at a steady volume.
• Browse by album, artist, genre or playlist, or search your whole library.
• A queue you control, with shuffle and repeat.
• Lock screen and Control Centre controls, and AirPlay to your speakers.
• Now Playing shows the format and audio quality of the song you are hearing.
• Light and dark appearance.
• Runs on iPhone and iPad.

FREE TRIAL, THEN PAY ONCE

Shuttle Music is free to download, and playing music stored on your device is always free. Streaming from your Jellyfin, Emby or Plex server is part of Shuttle Music Pro. The first time you play a song from your server, the app offers a 14-day free trial that you start with one tap, and after that Pro is a single one-off lifetime purchase. There is no subscription and no ads, and you can restore your purchases from the paywall or in Settings at any time.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN

Shuttle Music collects no data. There is no analytics and no crash reporting. Your server address and login stay on your device, in the Keychain, and are only ever sent to the server you chose. Purchases are handled by Apple.

MUSIC AND SERVERS

Shuttle Music does not provide music or a server. To play from the device you need your own audio files. To stream you need a Jellyfin, Emby or Plex server of your own, reachable from your iPhone or iPad, on your home network or over the internet.
