Your music, on your phone or your own server.

Shuttle Music plays the files on your device and streams from your own Jellyfin, Emby, Plex or Navidrome server. No ads, and no account with us.

Free to download. Playing music on your device is always free; streaming from your server is part of Shuttle Music Pro, with a free trial.

Where your music lives

One player for all of it.

Shuttle Music doesn't sell or stream music of its own. It plays your library: the files you keep on your device, and the media server you run at home or in the cloud.

Features

Built for people who care about their library.

Gapless playback

Albums play the way they were made, with no gap between tracks, including FLAC.

Equalizer and ReplayGain

Shape the sound with the equalizer, and keep a steady volume from song to song.

Browse and search

Albums, artists, genres, playlists and songs, with artwork and fast scrolling through big libraries.

A queue you control

Play next, reorder, shuffle and repeat. The queue is yours.

Know what you're hearing

Now Playing shows the format and audio quality of the song that's playing.

Light and dark

Follows your system appearance, day and night.

Pricing

Free to play. Pay once to stream.

No ads, ever. Pro unlocks streaming from your own server, and there's a free trial first.

Shuttle Music

Free
  • Play all the music stored on your device
  • Equalizer, ReplayGain and gapless playback
  • On Android: Chromecast, Android Auto, and songs you've already downloaded

Shuttle Music Pro

Free trial, then a one-off purchase
  • Stream from your Jellyfin, Emby or Plex server, and Navidrome on Android
  • On Android, download songs from your server for offline listening
  • Start the free trial the first time you play a song from your server: 14 days on Android, 7 days on iPhone and iPad
  • Then a single lifetime purchase. Android also offers a yearly plan.

Purchases go through Google Play or the App Store, and you can restore them in the app at any time. Music on your device, and songs you've downloaded, keep playing whether or not you have Pro.

Privacy

Your library stays yours.

There's no account with us. Your server address and login stay on your device and are only ever sent to the server you chose. The app sends anonymous crash reports and usage statistics that aren't linked to you and aren't used for tracking, and you can turn either off in Settings.
