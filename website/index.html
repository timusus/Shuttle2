Now Playing

The player takes its colours from the cover, in light or dark, so every album looks like itself.

  • The format and quality of what's playing, at a glance
  • Sleep timer and playback speed, a tap away

Queue

The queue does what you tell it.

Line up what's next, change your mind, and pick up where you left off.

  • Play next or add to the queue, then drag to reorder
  • Gapless playback, so live albums and mixes flow without a break
  • Your queue, shuffle and place are still there when you come back
Sound

Sound, tuned to taste.

Shape the sound once for every album, and keep the volume steady between records mastered loud and quiet.

  • A 10-band equalizer with presets, or your own curve
  • ReplayGain by track or by album, with a preamp
  • On Android: crossfade up to 12 seconds, and USB DAC output at each song's own sample rate (Android 14 and later)

Shuttle Music Pro

Free to play. Pro to stream.

Everything for the music on your phone is free, with no ads. Pro is for streaming from your server.

  • Stream your Jellyfin, Emby, Plex and Navidrome libraries
  • Download albums from your server for offline listening
  • Choose the streaming quality for Wi-Fi and for mobile data
  • Your phone's music and your server's, side by side
  • Buy once and keep it. Android also offers a yearly plan.

Try it free first: the trial starts the first time you play from a server, and lasts 14 days on Android and 7 days on iPhone and iPad. Purchases go through Google Play or the App Store.

FAQ

Before you ask.

Does Shuttle Music come with music?

No. It plays your own audio files, or music from a Jellyfin, Emby, Plex or Navidrome server that you run or have access to. It isn't a streaming service.

What's free, and what needs Pro?

Playing the music stored on your phone is free, with every feature. Pro adds streaming and downloads from your own server, and you can try it free for 14 days on Android or 7 days on iPhone and iPad.

Does the app collect data?

Only anonymous crash reports and usage statistics, which aren't linked to you, and you can turn each off in Settings. Your server login stays on your device. The privacy policy has the details.

Is this the original Shuttle Music Player?

Shuttle Music is a separate app from the original Shuttle Music Player, with its own Google Play listing.
