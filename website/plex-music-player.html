A Plex music player for Android

Play your Plex music library, next to the music on your phone.

Shuttle Music is an Android music player built around the music itself. Point it at your Plex server and your albums, artists and playlists appear in a library that also holds the files on your phone. I make it on my own, and it has no account of its own.

Connect your Plex music library

  1. Install Shuttle Music from Google Play.
  2. Open Settings, choose to add a source, and pick Plex.
  3. Sign in with your Plex account.
  4. Choose your server and its music library, then let the first sync finish.

Your Plex login stays on your device and is only sent to Plex and your server.

What works with your Plex music library

  • Streaming from your server, with FLAC and Opus playing natively.
  • Gapless playback, a reorderable queue, and your place saved between sessions.
  • A 10-band equalizer with presets, a preamp and ReplayGain by track or album.
  • Android Auto, Chromecast, home-screen widgets and a sleep timer.
  • Playlists and smart playlists, with your phone's own files in the same library.

Questions

Do I need Plex Pass?

Shuttle Music connects to your Plex server with your Plex account and doesn't ask for anything else. What your Plex account or server requires is up to Plex.

Can I use my phone's music and Plex together?

Yes. Local files and your Plex library appear in one library.

Tips for a good first sync

  • Sign in with the same Plex account that owns or has access to the server, then pick the server from the list Plex gives you.
  • Choose the library that holds your music. Plex libraries are separate for movies, shows and music, and Shuttle Music reads the music one.
  • The first sync of a big library takes a few minutes. You can start browsing while it finishes.
  • Titles, artists and artwork come from what Plex has matched, so if an album looks wrong, correct it in Plex and sync again.

Why use a dedicated Plex music app

As a Plex music app, Shuttle Music is built around the music, not the rest of a media server. You get a library laid out by album and artist, a queue you can reorder, a real equalizer, ReplayGain, a sleep timer, and playback in the car through Android Auto or on your speakers through Chromecast. Songs stored on your phone sit in the same library, so you don't change apps to change source.

FLAC and Opus play through Shuttle Music's own decoders, and playback is gapless, so live albums and mixes run on without a break. More on the FLAC player page.

Pricing

Shuttle Music comes with a 14-day trial of every feature, no purchase needed to start. After the trial, unlock it with a one-time purchase or a yearly plan. If you don't, playback gradually speeds up until you do.

Does it work away from home?

Yes, wherever your phone can reach your Plex server, whether that is through Plex's own remote access or a VPN you run.
