Getting started

The first time you open Shuttle Music it asks where your music is. You can add more places later in Settings > Sources, and use your phone and one or more servers side by side.

Music on your device

  • Allow access to your music when asked, and Shuttle Music finds the songs on your phone. You can also pick the folders it reads.
  • The iPhone app is coming soon.

Jellyfin

  • Enter your server's address, for example http://my.server.com:8096, then your username and password.

Emby

  • Enter your server's address and your Emby username and password.

Plex

  • Sign in with your plex.tv account, then choose your server and music library.

Servers

Which servers does Shuttle Music work with?

Jellyfin, Emby and Plex, on Android. The iPhone app is coming soon.

Can I use my phone's music and a server together?

Yes. Add each place your music lives in Settings > Sources, and they play side by side.

Where does my server login go?

It stays on your device and is only ever sent to your server. There's no account with us, and Plex sign-in goes through your plex.tv account. The privacy policy has the details.

Playback

Does Shuttle Music come with music?

No. It plays your own audio files, or music from a Jellyfin, Emby or Plex server that you run or have access to. It isn't a streaming service.

Which audio formats play?

FLAC, MP3, AAC and M4A, Ogg Vorbis, Opus and WAV.

Is playback gapless?

Yes, so albums that run from one track into the next play without a break.

Can I listen offline?

Music stored on your phone plays with no connection. Music from a Jellyfin, Emby or Plex server streams, so it needs a connection to your server.

Does it work in the car?

Yes, through Android Auto.

Pro and purchases

How much does Shuttle Music cost?

It comes with a 14-day trial of every feature. After the trial you unlock it with a one-time purchase or a yearly plan.

How do I restore a purchase?

Sign in to Google Play with the account you bought Pro with, then tap Restore purchases on the Shuttle Music Pro screen (in Settings).

How do I get a refund or cancel the yearly plan?

Google Play handles payment, so refunds and cancellations go through it, in the Play Store under Payments & subscriptions.

Troubleshooting

My server won't connect. What should I check?
  • Your phone has to reach the server: on the same Wi-Fi as a home server, or over the internet if you've made it available there. Try the same address in your phone's browser.
  • Include the port (:8096 for Jellyfin and Emby by default) and http:// or https:// as your server uses.
  • Plain http:// addresses on your home network are fine.
Some songs on my phone are missing. Why?

Check that Shuttle Music has access to your music and, if you picked folders, that the songs are inside them.

Is this the original Shuttle Music Player?

Shuttle Music is a separate app from the original Shuttle Music Player, with its own Google Play listing.
