Getting started

The first time you open Shuttle Music it asks where your music is. You can add more places later in Settings > Sources, and use your device and one or more servers side by side.

Music on your device (free)

  • Android: allow access to your music when asked, and Shuttle Music finds the songs on your phone. You can also pick the folders it reads.
  • iPhone and iPad: choose Use this device, then add songs to Shuttle Music's folder with the Files app or with Finder on a Mac, or pick a folder you already have.
  • FLAC, MP3, M4A, AAC, Ogg, Opus, WAV and AIFF all play.

Jellyfin

  • Enter your server's address, for example http://my.server.com:8096, then your username and password.
  • Or use Quick Connect: Shuttle Music shows a code; in Jellyfin's web app or another signed-in client, open your profile, then Quick Connect, and enter it.

Emby

  • Enter your server's address and your Emby username and password.

Plex

  • Sign in with your plex.tv account, then choose your server and music library.

Navidrome and other Subsonic servers (Android)

  • Enter your server's address, for example http://my.server.com:4533, and your username and password. OpenSubsonic servers can sign in with an API key instead.

If your server won't connect

  • Your phone has to reach the server: on the same Wi-Fi as a home server, or over the internet if you've made it available there. Try the same address in your phone's browser.
  • Include the port (:8096 for Jellyfin and Emby by default, :4533 for Navidrome) and http:// or https:// as your server uses.
  • Plain http:// addresses on your home network are fine.

Pro and purchases

Playing music stored on your device is free. Streaming from Jellyfin, Emby, Plex or Navidrome is part of Shuttle Music Pro. The first time you play a song from your server, the app offers a free trial: 14 days on Android, 7 days on iPhone and iPad. After that, Pro is a single lifetime purchase; Android also offers a yearly plan. When a trial ends without a purchase, server songs stop playing, while music on your device and songs you've already downloaded keep playing.

Restoring a purchase

  • Android: sign in to Google Play with the account you bought Pro with, then tap Restore purchases on the Shuttle Music Pro screen (in Settings).
  • iPhone and iPad: use the same Apple Account, then tap Restore purchases on the Pro screen or in Settings. The lifetime purchase works with Family Sharing.
  • A purchase on Google Play doesn't carry over to the App Store, or the other way round: the stores keep them separate.

Refunds and subscriptions

Google Play and the App Store handle payment, so refunds and yearly-plan cancellations go through them: in the Play Store, Payments & subscriptions; on iPhone, reportaproblem.apple.com.

Questions

Does Shuttle Music come with music?

No. It plays your own audio files, or music from a Jellyfin, Emby, Plex or Navidrome server that you run or have access to. It isn't a streaming service.

Do I need an account?

Not with us. You sign in to your own server, and Plex uses your plex.tv account. Your login stays on your device.

Does the app collect data?

Only anonymous crash reports and usage statistics, which aren't linked to you, and you can turn each off in Settings > Privacy. The privacy policy has the details.

Is this the original Shuttle Music Player?

Shuttle Music is a separate app from the original Shuttle Music Player, with its own Google Play listing.

Can I listen offline?

Music stored on your device always plays offline. On Android you can also download songs from your server to play without a connection.
