A FLAC player for Android

Lossless files from your phone or your server, played gaplessly.

Shuttle Music ships its own FLAC and Opus decoders, so lossless files play the same way across devices instead of depending on what a phone's firmware happens to support. It's a music player first: a library of albums and artists, a queue, and settings that stay out of the way. I make it on my own, and it has no ads.

Play your FLAC files

  1. Install Shuttle Music from Google Play.
  2. Let it scan your music, or choose the folders to use with Android's folder picker.
  3. Browse by album, artist or playlist and press play.

What works

  • FLAC and Opus through built-in decoders, alongside MP3, AAC, Ogg Vorbis and WAV.
  • Gapless playback between tracks.
  • ReplayGain by track or album, with a preamp, to even out loud and quiet masters.
  • A 10-band equalizer with presets and a frequency response graph.
  • A tag editor for fixing titles, artists and years on one song or many.
  • Streaming of the same formats from Jellyfin, Emby and Plex.

Limits

There is no bit-perfect or exclusive-output mode in the current version: audio goes through Android's normal audio system. Server songs stream, with no offline download. Shuttle Music is a paid app with a 14-day free trial of every feature, then a one-time purchase or a yearly plan.

Questions

Is playback bit-perfect?

No. Shuttle Music decodes FLAC properly and plays it through Android's audio system, but it doesn't offer a bit-perfect or exclusive-output mode in the current version.

Is FLAC playback gapless?

Yes, so live albums and mixes that run from one track into the next play without a break.

Can I stream FLAC from a server?

Yes, from Jellyfin, Emby and Plex. Offline downloads from a server aren't available in the current version.
