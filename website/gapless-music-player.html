A gapless music player for Android

Continuous playback, with no silence between tracks.

Some music isn't meant to stop between tracks: live albums, DJ mixes, classical works split into movements, concept records. Shuttle Music plays them gaplessly, so one track runs straight into the next. It does that for the files on your phone and for music streamed from your own server. I make it on my own.

Where gapless matters

  • Live albums, where the crowd carries on across the track break.
  • DJ mixes and continuous sets, split into tracks but meant to flow.
  • Classical music, where a symphony's movements shouldn't be separated by a pause.
  • Concept albums, where one song fades into the next.

Gapless from every source

  • Files on your phone: FLAC, MP3, AAC, Ogg Vorbis, Opus and WAV, with FLAC and Opus played through built-in decoders.
  • Streaming from Jellyfin, Emby and Plex.
  • Alongside the 10-band equalizer and Android Auto.

Getting clean transitions

  • Keep an album's tracks in order and tagged with the same album name, so they queue as one album. The tag editor can fix mismatched tags.
  • Start the album from its first track, or queue the whole album, so the next track is ready in the queue.
  • For streamed music, a steady connection to your server keeps the next track ready in time. Files on your phone don't depend on one.

Questions

What is gapless playback?

Gapless playback starts the next track the moment the current one ends, with no silence in between, so albums and mixes that flow from one track into the next play as the artist made them.

Does gapless work with Jellyfin, Emby and Plex?

Yes. Tracks streamed from Jellyfin, Emby and Plex play gaplessly, as long as the connection to your server keeps up.

Does gapless work with FLAC?

Yes. FLAC plays through Shuttle Music's own decoders, gaplessly.
