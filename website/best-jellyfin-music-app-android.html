Best Jellyfin music app for Android

Shuttle Music, Finamp, Symfonium and Findroid compared, by the person who makes Shuttle Music.

There is no single best Jellyfin app, because people want different things from one. I make Shuttle Music, so I have a stake here, and I've tried to describe the other three the way their own pages describe them. Details below come from each project's public GitHub or store page and were checked on 19 November 2026; apps change, so confirm anything that matters to you on those pages.

Shuttle Music

A music player for the files on your phone and for Jellyfin, Emby and Plex, in a single library. It has a 10-band equalizer, ReplayGain, gapless playback, its own FLAC and Opus decoders, a tag editor, Android Auto and Chromecast. It has no account of its own. It is a paid app with a 14-day trial; after the trial you unlock it with a one-time purchase or a yearly plan, and if you don't, playback gradually speeds up until you do. It suits people who want local files and a media server in one player. Set it up with Jellyfin.

Finamp

An open-source music client made only for Jellyfin, at no charge, with versions for Android, iOS and desktop. Its README lists offline downloads, gapless playback, ReplayGain-style volume normalisation, transcoded streaming to save mobile data, and Android Auto. It suits people who want a music-focused Jellyfin app at no charge, one they can inspect and contribute to. Source: github.com/jmshrv/finamp.

Symfonium

A paid Android app with a trial of every feature and then a one-time purchase. It is the broadest of the four: Jellyfin, Plex, Emby and other server types, plus local files, cloud storage and network shares. It lists offline caching, gapless playback, ReplayGain, Android Auto, Chromecast and parametric and graphic equalizers, and runs on Android TV and Wear OS too. It suits people who run several kinds of server or want deep control over sound. Source: symfonium.app.

Findroid

An open-source Android client for Jellyfin, licensed GPLv3. Its README describes a video-first app, listing movies, series and episodes, with offline downloads and direct play. It focuses on video, so it is a different kind of app from the other three, which are built around music. If your Jellyfin library is mostly movies and shows, it is worth a look. Source: github.com/jarnedemeulemeester/findroid.

Which should you pick?

  • No charge and Jellyfin-only: Finamp.
  • Many servers, or fine control of the sound: Symfonium.
  • Local files plus Jellyfin, Emby or Plex, with an equalizer and tag editor: Shuttle Music.
  • Movies and shows from Jellyfin: Findroid.

Most of these have a trial or cost nothing, so installing two and living with each for a week is a fair way to choose.
