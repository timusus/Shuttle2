An offline music player for Android

The music on your phone, with no internet or signal needed.

Shuttle Music plays the music files stored on your phone, so a flight, a train through a tunnel or a trip abroad doesn't stop the music. Nothing in the player needs a connection to play your own files. I make it on my own, and it has no account of its own to sign in to.

Listen with no signal

  1. Put your music on the phone as files, over USB or by copying it from your computer.
  2. Install Shuttle Music from Google Play and let it scan your music, or choose the folders to use with Android's folder picker.
  3. Browse by album, artist or playlist and press play. Turn on airplane mode if you like.

What works offline

  • FLAC, MP3, AAC, Ogg Vorbis, Opus and WAV from your phone's storage.
  • The 10-band equalizer with presets and a preamp.
  • Gapless playback, so albums that run on from one track to the next don't pause.
  • Android Auto, for the files on your phone, in a car with no data connection.
  • A queue you can reorder, playlists, and a sleep timer for the night flight.
  • The tag editor, to tidy titles and artists before you travel.

Before a flight or a trip

  • Check the albums you want are on the phone itself, not only on a server or a cloud drive.
  • Open the app once to make sure the library is up to date, so new folders are picked up.
  • If you use ReplayGain, switch it on now. Loud and quiet records then play at a similar level through earphones.

Servers need a connection

If your collection lives on Jellyfin, Emby or Plex, Shuttle Music streams it from your server, which needs a connection to reach it. It doesn't download songs from a server for later. For a trip, keep a copy of the music you want on your phone.

Trial and purchase

Shuttle Music comes with a 14-day trial of every feature, no purchase needed to start. After the trial, unlock it with a one-time purchase or a yearly plan. If you don't, playback gradually speeds up until you do.

Questions

Does Shuttle Music work without internet?

Yes, for the music files stored on your phone. They play with no internet and no signal, so airplane mode is fine.

Can I download songs from my server to listen offline?

No. Jellyfin, Emby and Plex libraries stream, so they need a connection to your server. For a flight or a trip, put the music on your phone as files.

Does the equalizer work offline?

Yes. The equalizer, gapless playback and Android Auto don't depend on a connection when the music is on your phone.

Which formats play offline?

FLAC, MP3, AAC, Ogg Vorbis, Opus and WAV.
